How it works

  1. Browse the catalog Every item shows its price in Kenyan shillings and how many are in stock.
  2. Filter and total your order Search by name, narrow by category or price, then set quantities to see your total.
  3. Create your account Register once to buy and sell with other students.

CampusMarket is a course project for CCS 2314 Web Based Programming II. All prices are in Kenyan shillings (KES).
